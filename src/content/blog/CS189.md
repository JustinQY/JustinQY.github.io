# CS189 Notes

## Classifiers

### Perceptron Algorithm

> Inequality called a constraint.

#### Main Logic

Define a **risk function** $R$ that is positive if some constraints are violated, then use **optimization** to choose $w$ that **minimizes** $R$.

#### Feature space and Weights space

What we're trying to do is to find a hyperplane in the x-space (feature space). But we now transform it into a problem of finding an optimal point in the w-space (weight space).

> A point in x-space can transform to a hyperplane (line in 2D space) in w-space, and all points in x-space can form a feasible region in w-space.
> Any point in w-space can transform to a hyperplane in x-space. Because w is the normal vector of the hyperplane in x-space, the hyperplane can separate the points in x-space.

#### stochastic gradient descent

Each time pick **one misclassified point** $X_i$, do gradient descent on $R$.

### Maximum Margin Classifiers

Margin: the distance from the decision boundary to the nearest training point.

#### Optimization problem

Maximize the margin, which is equivalent to minimizing $\|w\|$ subject to the constraints $y_i(w^T x_i + b) \geq 1$ for all training points.

> It's a quadratic optimization problem in d+1 dimensions and n constraints.
> If points are linearly separable, it has one unique solution. Otherwise no solution.
